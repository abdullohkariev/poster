# External context sources

Checked 29 October 2026. These sources support the optional “Afghanistan: Beyond One Story” section and are kept separate from memoir evidence.

1. **UNESCO, 11 August 2026 (updated 19 August 2026)** — “Five years on: UNESCO calls for the full restoration of the right to education for Afghan girls and women.” UNESCO estimates that **2.4 million girls remain excluded from secondary education as of 2026**. This is the main on-page statistic and is labelled with its reporting date.  
   https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/five-years-unesco-calls-full-restoration-right-education-afghan-girls-and-women

2. **UNICEF, 14 August 2026** — “More than 2.6 million girls in Afghanistan have been denied a secondary education since 2021.” UNICEF reports a cumulative five-year figure and states that girls have been barred from formal education beyond grade six since September 2021 and women from universities since December 2022.  
   https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/more-26-million-girls-afghanistan-have-been-denied-secondary-education-2021

   The UNICEF 2.6 million figure is not placed beside UNESCO’s 2.4 million as a competing statistic. They describe different measures: a cumulative population denied education over five years versus an estimated current excluded population.

3. **International Islamic Fiqh Academy, Resolution 239, 28 July 2023** — The resolution states that beneficial education is a right for males and females at all levels and rejects depriving either sex of beneficial education. The website paraphrases this carefully as one institutional scholarly position, not universal agreement on every religious or political issue.  
   https://iifa-aifi.org/en/49686.html
