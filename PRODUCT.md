# WHO ARE YOU?
## Register
brand
## Platform
web
## Users
School students scanning a hallway poster; older students and teachers exploring literary evidence.
## Product Purpose
A gentle interactive exhibition about identity, choices, voice and empathy, grounded in supplied memoir scans.
## Positioning
Understand circumstances before judging choices. Silence can be survival.
## Brand Personality
Thoughtful, humane, cinematic. Restrained charcoal, deep red and warm-neutral editorial system; original symbolic SVG art.
## Conversion & proof
Two-minute journey or deeper exploration, ending in a private reflection card. Proof comes from verified printed book pages.
## Accessibility
Keyboard access, comfortable text, reduced motion, mobile-first, no tracking or public submissions.
