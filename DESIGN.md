# Design
Approved palette: cream #F6F1E8, ink #202523, green #263F3B, terracotta #A85C49, sage #C7D0C2, sand #E8D9C5, white.
Students open the site on phones in a bright school hallway: clear light surfaces, strong contrast and generous touch targets.
Literary serif headlines paired with a clean system sans. Architectural symbolic portrait; no historical or documentary image claims.
Wide editorial hero, ruled chapter sequence, dark-green scenario stage, expanding theme rows and cream reflection card. No decorative shadows or glass.
Motion: short entrance and button transitions; reduced-motion override. Local fonts/fallbacks; no runtime external requests.
