# Design
Version 2 palette: warm background #F3F0EB, secondary #E4E0DA, charcoal #1D2022, deep red #842F35, slate #686C70, dark red #60262D, white.
Students open the site on phones in a bright school hallway: clear light surfaces, strong contrast and generous touch targets.
Literary serif headlines paired with a clean system sans. Architectural symbolic portrait; no historical or documentary image claims.
Wide editorial hero, a single charcoal cinematic opening, ruled chapter progression, charcoal scenario stage, expandable theme rows, dark-red context section and result-accented reflection card. No decorative shadows or glass.
Motion: one session-scoped opening plus short state transitions; reduced-motion override. Local fonts/fallbacks. Only user-activated external source links create network requests.
